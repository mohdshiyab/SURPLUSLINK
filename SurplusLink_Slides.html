The Challenge

Why Traditional Food Donation Platforms Fail

📍

Navigation Friction

Volunteers get vague text addresses like "behind the market". Food spoils and gets cold while drivers wander trying to find actual kitchen pickup doors.

👻

Ghost Claims & Hoarding

Unregulated systems let users claim more food than their car holds, or book conflicting pickup hours across the city, leading to uncollected food waste.

📉

Zero Accountability

Charity apps lack mathematical auditing. There is no cryptographic or algorithmic proof of whether food was collected, lost in transit, or discarded.

Architecture Overview

SurplusLink: Multi-Rule Closed-Loop Ecosystem

1. Interactive GPS Geolocation
Leaflet map pins + native Google Maps turn-by-turn routing.
2. Strict 7-Rule Claim Engine
Capacity checks, 60-min buffers, and atomic reservation concurrency locks.
3. Zero-Leakage Conservation Invariant
Live mathematical proof: Listed = Collected + Missed + Wasted + Active + Open.
4. Authoritative Simulated Clock
Deterministic time control allowing instant 1-click evaluation of deadlines.

Closed-Loop Role Lifecycles

🏢 Restaurant Donors (D1–D5):

Pins GPS location, posts surplus batches with packaging type & time window.

🚴 Volunteer Rescuers (V1–V6):

Discovers nearby food, claims within capacity, navigates via Google Maps, delivers to shelters.

🛡️ NGO Coordinator (COORD1):

Monitors citywide operations, audits the zero-leakage invariant, rewards community heroes.

The Rule Engine

The 7 Official Claim Rules (Zero Ghost Bookings)

Rule 1: Donation Status
Must be OPEN (not cancelled, expired, or fully claimed).
Rule 2: Vehicle Capacity
Claimed kg ≤ volunteer's registered capacity (whole kg).
Rule 3: Remaining Quantity
Claimed kg ≤ donation's remaining unreserved quantity.
Rule 4: Zone Eligibility
Restaurant zone must be included in volunteer's zones.
Rule 5: Pickup Window Window
Planned pickup T must be within [pickup_from, pickup_until].
Rule 6: No Past Planning
Planned pickup T cannot be before simulated now (T ≥ now).
Rule 7: 60-Minute Conflict Buffer
Planned pickup T must be ≥ 60 minutes away from every other active or collected claim of that volunteer, preventing cross-city scheduling impossibilities.
Mathematical Integrity

The Zero-Leakage Conservation Equation

The Invariant Law
Total Listed (kg) = Collected + Missed + Wasted + In Progress + Still Open
Collected
Successfully picked up & delivered to shelters.
Missed
Claimed, but volunteer failed to collect before deadline.
Wasted
Remaining unclaimed kg on expired donations.
In Progress
Currently reserved under active volunteer claims.
Still Open
Unreserved food available for volunteers to claim right now.
Determinism & Testing

Authoritative Simulated Clock Architecture

⏱️

Why System Time Was Banned

  • Deterministic Evaluation: Judges cannot wait 4 real-world hours to test evening pickup windows.
  • Zero Test Flakiness: Tests run against predictable timestamps without timezone discrepancies.
  • Forward-Only Guarantee: Clock strictly advances (+15m, +1h, +4h) and can never move backwards.
  • Automated Missed Transitions: Advancing past pickup deadline automatically penalties uncollected claims.
⚙️

Clock State Machine Implementation

  • MongoDB Persisted Clock: State is centralized in Atlas DB and synced across all browser tabs.
  • React Context Provider: Dynamic UI updates buttons live as the clock crosses pickup thresholds.
  • Persistent Top Banner: Evaluators can advance time with 1 click from any screen.
  • Backend Middleware: Every claim endpoint queries getSimulatedNow() for authoritative validation.
